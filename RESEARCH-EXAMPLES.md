# Research examples for the corpus collection

[Executed six-workstream update and eighteen reproducible walkthroughs](references/nonreview-work-2026-10-27.md). The original academic closure criteria remain unchanged.

These exercises show how to use the master and individual skills to investigate a focused research question: assemble a critical apparatus, test the stability of a pattern, expose circular reasoning or identify evidence that would discriminate hypotheses. They are **proposed research workflows, not executed studies or reported discoveries**. Availability of the required inputs must be checked against the current native corpus. No example promises a complete dataset, primary-edition access or a resolved interpretation.

Complete [the user guide setup](USER-GUIDE.md#set-up-your-first-session) first. The prompts below assume the master instructions, selected individual skill and a declared evidence packet are accessible. For saved executions and numerical acceptance criteria, use [the separate worked demonstrations](WORKED-EXAMPLES.md).

For comparisons involving more than one project, start with [five cross-corpus walkthroughs](CROSS-CORPUS-EXAMPLES.md): administrative evidence, bilingual contexts, overlapping records, sign position and formula variation.

## Use the same research sequence for each example

1. **Scope:** supply the stated inputs; ask the assistant to inventory accessible evidence and missing prerequisites.
2. **Retrieve:** discover native collections and retrieve exact records. Preserve original notation and source attribution.
3. **Analyze:** use the project prompt to build an evidence table and a method. Perform a calculation only on declared inputs with recorded execution.
4. **Challenge:** test the effect of source dependence, uncertainty and editorial choices. Treat an unmet prerequisite as a research finding, not an invitation to guess.
5. **Deliver:** save the proposed output with the [research log](USER-GUIDE.md#check-and-cite-the-result). Have a specialist assess the substantive claim.

In the prompts, a “table” requests a new analytical presentation of accessible evidence, not an assertion that a native file with that name already exists. A requested field may be missing. Return `not supplied` and its consequence; never synthesize a source, inscription or numerical outcome.

## 1. Linear A

**Research question:** Are apparent terminal sequences robust to editorial and segmentation choices?

**Select:** [Linear-A](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Linear-A) and its [individual skill](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Linear-A/blob/main/ai-skill/SKILL.md).

**Evidence to supply or establish:** An admitted set of source records with positional information, original sign identifiers, segmentation conventions, damage/restoration flags and source links. ARKH 1a can teach record tracing but cannot establish a population-level pattern.

**Copy this research prompt:**

```text
I am testing whether a selected Linear A sequence is disproportionately terminal. First establish how my packet defines a sequence and a terminal position. Preserve source sign labels without treating them as established phonetic values. Build a concordance with document, source slot, boundary convention and uncertainty. Propose a within-document or otherwise justified null model and an analysis excluding restored and boundary-ambiguous cases. Do not produce a test statistic until inputs and execution are available.
```

**Deliverable and interpretation boundary:** Produce a concordance, explicit denominator, executable analysis plan and sensitivity table. A result may support a positional regularity in this sample; it does not establish a suffix, sound value or decipherment.

<a id="example-2"></a>
## 2. Cypro-Minoan

**Research question:** Does a proposed distribution reflect writing practice or the composition of the sample?

**Select:** [Cypro-Minoan](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Cypro-Minoan) and its [individual skill](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Cypro-Minoan/blob/main/ai-skill/SKILL.md).

**Evidence to supply or establish:** Records with recorded provenance, object/support categories, sign representations and source attribution. Check whether archaeological contexts, dating and sign-system assignments are actually supplied.

**Copy this research prompt:**

```text
My study examines whether a recurring recorded sign pattern varies across supports. Inventory accessible support and provenance fields before selecting the sample. Preserve disputed sign identifications and corpus classifications. Separate support, site, chronology and source edition as possible confounders. Design stratified descriptive comparisons and identify cells too small or poorly documented for inference. Never turn a sign label into a pronunciation.
```

**Deliverable and interpretation boundary:** Produce a sample audit and stratified table with missingness and dependence. Without comparable strata, report the confounding problem and an acquisition plan rather than a historical explanation.

<a id="example-3"></a>
## 3. Linear B

**Research question:** Can an administrative distribution survive restoration and archive effects?

**Select:** [Linear-B](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Linear-B) and its [individual skill](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Linear-B/blob/main/ai-skill/SKILL.md).

**Evidence to supply or establish:** Admitted tablets or entries for a bounded administrative question, diplomatic and normalized forms where available, restoration flags, source attribution and independently supported archive/context metadata.

**Copy this research prompt:**

```text
I want to evaluate an administrative distribution in a bounded Linear B sample. Separate what the tablet records, what editors restore and what scholars interpret. Define the accounting or textual unit before counting. Audit fragments, joins and duplicate editions. Compare the proposed distribution with and without restored readings and by archive where supported. Identify which supplied interpretations have verified scholarly citations and which remain hypotheses.
```

**Deliverable and interpretation boundary:** Produce a source-linked administrative concordance and a sensitivity analysis. Do not infer the whole palatial economy from a selected view or claim that a native calibration gate has been completed unless its evidence establishes this.

<a id="example-4"></a>
## 4. Cretan Hieroglyphic

**Research question:** Do sign-position patterns change with the inscription-bearing support?

**Select:** [Cretan-hieroglyphs](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Cretan-hieroglyphs) and its [individual skill](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Cretan-hieroglyphs/blob/main/ai-skill/SKILL.md).

**Evidence to supply or establish:** Selected source-backed records with support, sign order, layout/orientation, segmentation and uncertainty. Obtain permitted images or source observations before any palaeographic test.

**Copy this research prompt:**

```text
I am studying whether recorded sign positions differ between support categories. Establish whether order and orientation are comparable in my packet. Keep catalogue sign identity separate from visual identification. Audit repeated impressions, seal matrices and editions before defining independent observations. Propose an analysis restricted to compatible layouts; describe excluded and indeterminate cases. Treat visual similarity as a separate image-based question.
```

**Deliverable and interpretation boundary:** Produce a support/layout eligibility matrix and a reproducible positional concordance. Repeated impressions may not be independent compositions; no phonetic or functional interpretation follows automatically.

<a id="example-5"></a>
## 5. Phaistos Disc

**Research question:** Which claims about repeated groups depend on boundary and orientation assumptions?

**Select:** [Phaistos-Disc](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Phaistos-Disc) and its [individual skill](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Phaistos-Disc/blob/main/ai-skill/SKILL.md).

**Evidence to supply or establish:** The corpus source representations of both faces, recorded group boundaries and orientation conventions, uncertainties and edition provenance; images only where access and reuse permit.

**Copy this research prompt:**

```text
My study critiques distributional arguments about the Phaistos Disc. Inventory the source-supported segmentation and orientation conventions. For each proposed repeat, cite the face and native locus. Distinguish repeated sign tokens, repeated groups and repetitions within one artefact. Propose sensitivity checks only for documented alternative representations, with a declared null model. Do not search many models and present the best one as confirmatory.
```

**Deliverable and interpretation boundary:** Produce an assumption ledger, repeat concordance and analysis plan. This is one artefact; internal repetition does not create independent texts or establish a language or translation.

<a id="example-6"></a>
## 6. Byblos syllabary

**Research question:** How stable is a sign inventory across recorded editorial classifications?

**Select:** [Byblos-syllabary](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Byblos-syllabary) and its [individual skill](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Byblos-syllabary/blob/main/ai-skill/SKILL.md).

**Evidence to supply or establish:** Available sign catalogues and source-linked inscription records, editorial correspondences and permitted illustrations/observations. A bibliography or unavailable edition is not an inspected primary source.

**Copy this research prompt:**

```text
I am assessing how editorial grouping affects the apparent Byblos sign inventory. Separate source identifiers from proposed allographs and grapheme assignments. Build an assertion table showing which editor or source supports each equivalence and at what locus. Flag correspondences lacking inspectable support. If compatible inventories are available, propose sensitivity counts under explicitly documented groupings. Do not reconstruct an unavailable restricted catalogue.
```

**Deliverable and interpretation boundary:** Produce an attributed inventory crosswalk with unresolved joins. Different catalogue counts need not demonstrate ancient variation; a grapheme inventory requires palaeographic and scholarly adjudication.

<a id="example-7"></a>
## 7. Cypriot syllabic Greek

**Research question:** Does an orthographic pattern remain after restoration and normalization are separated?

**Select:** [Cypriot-syllabic-Greek](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Cypriot-syllabic-Greek) and its [individual skill](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Cypriot-syllabic-Greek/blob/main/ai-skill/SKILL.md).

**Evidence to supply or establish:** Source-backed syllabic representations, scholarly transliterations and restorations, plus secure date/provenance and genre fields where recorded.

**Copy this research prompt:**

```text
I am studying an orthographic pattern in a bounded Cypriot syllabic Greek sample. Show the source representation, transliteration, normalization and restoration in separate columns. Define eligible tokens and alternative analyses before counting. Audit whether apparent regional differences instead follow date, genre, editorial normalization or source coverage. Compare only strata supported by the packet and cite supplied readings.
```

**Deliverable and interpretation boundary:** Produce an orthographic concordance and a confounder/missingness table. Readability does not remove editorial uncertainty, and a Greek interpretation cannot be exported to Eteocypriot.

<a id="example-8"></a>
## 8. Eteocypriot

**Research question:** What does a bilingual context constrain without yielding a word-for-word translation?

**Select:** [Eteocypriot](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Eteocypriot) and its [individual skill](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Eteocypriot/blob/main/ai-skill/SKILL.md).

**Evidence to supply or establish:** The selected source-linked texts, language/side assignments, editorial representations and documented physical or publication association. Require independent evidence for alignment.

**Copy this research prompt:**

```text
I am evaluating a proposed Eteocypriot interpretation using a bilingual context. Separate association of texts on an object from alignment of passages or words. Identify what the writing system permits us to transcribe and what remains linguistically unresolved. List constraints supported by the sources and multiple interpretations compatible with them. Do not infer equal length, equivalent syntax or a lexicon from parallel presentation.
```

**Deliverable and interpretation boundary:** Produce a constraint table and a ranked evidence-acquisition plan, not an invented translation. Record a proposed correspondence as a hypothesis with its actual scholarly source.

<a id="example-9"></a>
## 9. Eteocretan

**Research question:** Is a proposed recurring ending secure across readings and segmentation?

**Select:** [Eteocretan](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Eteocretan) and its [individual skill](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Eteocretan/blob/main/ai-skill/SKILL.md).

**Evidence to supply or establish:** Exact source passages, original script/transcription distinctions, damage/restoration marks, segmentation alternatives and source-backed dating where available.

**Copy this research prompt:**

```text
My study tests a proposed recurring ending in Eteocretan. Make an occurrence table with native loci, preserved versus restored letters and all supplied segmentations. State the sample searched, including negative search results as view-limited. Compare the recurrence under each documented segmentation and assess source dependence. Distinguish use of a readable alphabet from understanding the language.
```

**Deliverable and interpretation boundary:** Produce an attributed occurrence dossier and sensitivity summary. Orthographic recurrence may survive while a morphological or genealogical claim remains unsupported.

## 10. Lycian and Carian

**Research question:** Which apparent correspondences are comparable across languages and editions?

**Select:** [Lycian-and-Carian](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Lycian-and-Carian) and its [individual skill](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Lycian-and-Carian/blob/main/ai-skill/SKILL.md).

**Evidence to supply or establish:** Explicitly language-tagged records with diplomatic readings, scholarly interpretations, source loci and justified comparison contexts. Keep Lycian, Carian and any Milyan material separately identified.

**Copy this research prompt:**

```text
I am testing a proposed correspondence between Lycian and Carian formulae. Audit the language labels, reading authority, restoration and genre of each candidate before comparing. Identify whether the match is graphical, transliterational, lexical or structural. Require independently supported readings and a justified linguistic method for lexical claims. Report source and physical-witness dependence, including possible overlap with Milyan-Lycian-B.
```

**Deliverable and interpretation boundary:** Produce a comparison eligibility table and an attributed formula dossier. Repository co-location does not authorize pooling languages or treating similar Latin transliterations as cognates.

## 11. Phrygian

**Research question:** Is formula variation historical, or an artefact of source and genre selection?

**Select:** [Phrygian](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Phrygian) and its [individual skill](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Phrygian/blob/main/ai-skill/SKILL.md).

**Evidence to supply or establish:** A bounded, source-linked formula sample with recorded dating, provenance and genre, including edition variants and damage. Determine which period distinctions are actually supported.

**Copy this research prompt:**

```text
I am studying variation in a Phrygian formula. Establish the eligible sample and the source authority for each reading. Separate period, genre, provenance and editorial convention; do not infer a period from the formula being tested. Preserve uncertain letters and supplied alternatives. Propose a comparison excluding restored occurrences and assess whether the remaining strata support an inference.
```

**Deliverable and interpretation boundary:** Produce a formula concordance, sample-flow record and sensitivity analysis. An underpowered or confounded sample supports a documented limitation, not a narrative of language change.

<a id="example-12"></a>
## 12. Anatolian Hieroglyphic

**Research question:** How do editorial sign readings affect an interpretation of a Luwian passage?

**Select:** [Anatolian-Hieroglyphic](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Anatolian-Hieroglyphic) and its [individual skill](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Anatolian-Hieroglyphic/blob/main/ai-skill/SKILL.md).

**Evidence to supply or establish:** An admitted passage with exact loci, sign identifiers, attributed transliteration alternatives and supplied scholarly interpretation. Primary-edition or image consultation is additional work unless documented.

**Copy this research prompt:**

```text
I am examining an interpretation of a hieroglyphic Luwian passage. Preserve the distinction between sign identifier, reading value, transliteration, restoration and translation. Build a same-locus apparatus of the alternatives actually supplied and trace each to its authority. Describe how each alternative changes the argument without choosing solely because one yields a smoother translation. List the source collation needed to adjudicate it.
```

**Deliverable and interpretation boundary:** Produce a passage apparatus and an argument-dependency table. Do not treat catalogue identifiers as phonetic readings or extend Luwian readings to unrelated or undeciphered scripts.

## 13. Archanes Script

**Research question:** Can an attribution be defended without counting the same evidence twice?

**Select:** [Archanes-Script](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Archanes-Script) and its [individual skill](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Archanes-Script/blob/main/ai-skill/SKILL.md).

**Evidence to supply or establish:** Candidate records, exact source assertions about classification, recorded provenance and source/witness relationships; permitted observations for any graphical comparison.

**Copy this research prompt:**

```text
My chapter examines a proposed script attribution for an Archanes item. Inventory the item-level claims and their sources before adopting a classification. Check for overlap with Linear A and Cretan Hieroglyphic records using documented joins only. Separate findspot, editorial attribution and script identity. What additional observations would distinguish the proposed classifications? Do not manufacture an identity match from a shared label.
```

**Deliverable and interpretation boundary:** Produce an attribution dossier and an overlap register, marking unknown joins. Unresolved classification is a valid result; multiple repository records are not multiple archaeological witnesses.

## 14. Aegean Anomalous

**Research question:** Is an anomalous group coherent enough to analyze as one population?

**Select:** [Aegean-anomalous](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Aegean-anomalous) and its [individual skill](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Aegean-anomalous/blob/main/ai-skill/SKILL.md).

**Evidence to supply or establish:** The candidate inventory, item-level source assertions, inclusion/exclusion rules and available contextual observations. Treat classification and authenticity claims as source-attributed unless established.

**Copy this research prompt:**

```text
I am assessing whether an Aegean anomalous grouping is analytically coherent. Inventory the grounds for including each item and the uncertainties in its script attribution. Separate item-level provenance, recorded observations and modern interpretation. Identify possible overlaps with other registered projects. Propose explicit criteria for splitting the group before any frequency or historical analysis.
```

**Deliverable and interpretation boundary:** Produce a heterogeneity and eligibility matrix. A residual catalogue category does not establish one script, language, date or transmission network.

## 15. Lydian

**Research question:** Does a putative formula reflect preserved evidence or restoration convention?

**Select:** [Lydian](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Lydian) and its [individual skill](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Lydian/blob/main/ai-skill/SKILL.md).

**Evidence to supply or establish:** A source-linked set of passages with diplomatic text, editorial restorations, supplied translation/interpretation and documented genre or context.

**Copy this research prompt:**

```text
I am testing a proposed Lydian formula. Extract the passages and source loci without silently normalizing uncertain letters. Distinguish attested, partially restored and wholly supplied components. Audit whether repeated scholarly assertions derive from the same edition. Compare the formula's apparent distribution with restored cases excluded, and state which interpretations depend on external scholarship not present in the packet.
```

**Deliverable and interpretation boundary:** Produce an attributed formula table and a restoration-dependence report. Similar editorial supplements cannot serve as independent ancient attestations.

<a id="example-16"></a>
## 16. Sidetic

**Research question:** What evidence would discriminate competing sign-value proposals?

**Select:** [Sidetic](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Sidetic) and its [individual skill](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Sidetic/blob/main/ai-skill/SKILL.md).

**Evidence to supply or establish:** Exact sign occurrences, alternative value proposals with attributed source loci, source-independent constraints and accessible context. A purchased or uploaded article does not authorize redistribution.

**Copy this research prompt:**

```text
My study evaluates competing Sidetic sign-value proposals. Build an assertion table linking each proposed value to the actual source and supporting occurrence. Keep sign identity, phonetic proposal, transcription and lexical interpretation separate. Identify circularity where a name or translation is both premise and result. Propose discriminating observations and held-out tests if independent evidence exists; otherwise list what must be acquired.
```

**Deliverable and interpretation boundary:** Produce a hypothesis/evidence matrix and a falsification plan. No proposal becomes established merely because the AI can produce plausible words or because later sources repeat it.

## 17. Pisidian

**Research question:** Can a personal-name pattern survive language assignment and dependence checks?

**Select:** [Pisidian](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Pisidian) and its [individual skill](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Pisidian/blob/main/ai-skill/SKILL.md).

**Evidence to supply or establish:** Source-linked inscriptions or onomastic records, language-assignment evidence, name segmentation/reading authority, uncertain letters and contextual metadata.

**Copy this research prompt:**

```text
I am examining a proposed Pisidian personal-name pattern. Separate the script used, language attribution and onomastic interpretation. Build a concordance preserving alternative readings and uncertain name boundaries. Audit repeated persons, duplicate editions and formulaic dependence before defining the sampling unit. State what the packet can support about the recorded sample, and which broader linguistic claims need independent evidence.
```

**Deliverable and interpretation boundary:** Produce a name concordance and a language-assignment audit. Personal names alone do not establish productive morphology, ethnicity or the language of every associated text.

## 18. Milyan (Lycian B)

**Research question:** Does a structural pattern depend on editorial lineation or overlap with another dataset?

**Select:** [Milyan-Lycian-B](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Milyan-Lycian-B) and its [individual skill](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Milyan-Lycian-B/blob/main/ai-skill/SKILL.md).

**Evidence to supply or establish:** Source-linked passages with original lineation, documented editorial segmentation, language labels and asserted physical/source joins to related repositories.

**Copy this research prompt:**

```text
I am investigating a proposed structural pattern in Milyan. Separate physical lineation, editorial segmentation and proposed metrical or linguistic units. Inventory alternative representations actually documented in the packet. Check potential overlap with Lycian-and-Carian without assuming record identity. Design sensitivity analyses for the documented segmentation choices; mark any metrical inference as dependent on independently supported linguistic premises.
```

**Deliverable and interpretation boundary:** Produce a structural concordance, segmentation ledger and cross-repository overlap audit. Lycian B naming does not license unexamined transfer of Lycian analyses, and duplicate hosting is not replication.

## A cross-corpus research exercise: audit a claimed parallel

**Problem:** a proposed parallel appears in two registered projects. Is it a real analytical correspondence, duplicate source material, or merely the result of modern representation?

Select two of the examples above. Retrieve each project's evidence independently and read [the comparability matrix](references/comparability-matrix.md). Do not begin by pooling records.

```text
I am auditing a claimed parallel between [explicit corpus/record A] and
[explicit corpus/record B]. First document each native unit, source chain,
representation layer, chronology and uncertainty. Establish whether the records
are independent or could describe the same object/source. Classify the proposed
parallel as graphical, positional, lexical or another explicit claim type.
State whether its prerequisites are met. Where they are not, provide a bounded
side-by-side description and the evidence needed to proceed. Do not transfer
sound values, infer cognacy or assert borrowing from a literal string match.
```

**Deliverable:** a comparison eligibility decision with source-linked observations, documented joins or unknown joins, competing explanations and a plan for the missing evidence. A blocked historical claim can accompany a successful provenance audit. A frequency comparison additionally needs compatible units, sampling, denominators and a justified null model; the retrieval result alone supplies none of these approvals.

## From the packet to a research paragraph

After you have checked the sources and executed any analysis, ask:

```text
Draft a methods paragraph using only the verified research packet. State the
sample, revision, sources consulted, exclusions, dependence assumptions,
restoration/segmentation rules and executed methods. Then draft a separate
results paragraph limited to observed outputs. Mark interpretations and missing
checks explicitly. Flag any citation locator I still need to verify. Do not
invent a bibliography or imply expert review. Give the strongest defensible
claim and one plausible alternative explanation for a specialist to assess.
```

These exercises do not substitute for a literature review, primary-source collation or expert feedback. Their purpose is to make the path from a research question to the evidence inspectable, so that a specialist can challenge the argument at a precise point.
