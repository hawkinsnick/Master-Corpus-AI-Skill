These counts describe frozen eDiAna directory snapshots, not complete language inventories or independently verified objects. Other corpus members retain their native review procedures; this packet format has been implemented for the three members above.

Each page gives the captured reading, source pointers, edition citations, linked source checks and unresolved questions. Restricted source images and publications are linked rather than bundled. Source checking is not independent epigraphic review.

For an AI-assisted review request, provide the master starter, the member instructions, its handoff manifest, selected packet, source checks and relevant source evidence. Ask:

> Prepare a specialist review request for [record IDs]. Confirm what evidence you accessed. Give the exact record hashes and evidence fingerprint, edition page/line/figure locators, competing readings, shared source lineage and unanswered questions. Separate source inspection from independent review. Do not decide missing readings or invent a reviewer.

The expert records object identity, reading, uncertainty, classification and redistribution rights separately, with a rationale and inspected source locator. A decision may support one dimension and dispute another. Preserve negative and inconclusive findings. The native decision template and validator bind submissions to the evidence revision. Structural validation cannot establish qualifications or scientific correctness. It never changes admission automatically.

For a collection handoff, save the repository commit or release, handoff manifest fingerprint and chosen record hashes for each language. A review against an older fingerprint must be rechecked against any changed evidence. Keep the review submission separate from a later documented correction or admission.

Before describing the collection as ready for exhaustive expert validation, complete or explicitly disposition the remaining acquisition and collation tasks in each member's source-access log and pre-expert boundary file. The existing packets can support targeted review now; they do not imply that every primary edition has been acquired.
