## Dependence screening and dispositions

The [full receipt](../research/2026-10-25/retrieval-and-overlap.json) preserves source paths, hashes, exact field pointers and 130 dispositions without redistributing text. Screening examines fields literally named `text`, `reading`, `sentence` or `transliteration`, using Unicode NFC and whitespace collapse only. It retains case, punctuation, damage and editorial symbols. Only strings of at least24 normalized characters and four whitespace tokens qualify. Multiple views at the same source path/pointer within a repository are collapsed.

These choices deliberately miss short strings, fuzzy matches, translations across languages and source histories expressed without matching text. They do not test semantic equivalence. Every returned group was checked against raw upstream bindings: shared IG source identity/XML hash or shared Milyan cache hash/source-row locus. Those130 source-field groups are not130 ancient objects,114 complete Milyan rows or25 complete IG entries. The already published source unions remain the controlled tools for their own declared units. No-match relationships remain unknown.
